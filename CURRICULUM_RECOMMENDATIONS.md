# Curriculum Recommendations

**Date:** 2026-09-28  
**Principle:** Progress Foundations → Physiology → Pathophysiology → Pharmacology → Organ-system medicine → Clinical presentation → Reasoning → Diagnosis → Management → Cases → Assessment → Spaced review.  
**Constraint:** Recommendations first. Execute only safe, non-destructive changes unless a qualified reviewer directs otherwise.

---

## Global recommendations

1. **Tag every lesson** with `learner_level` and show badges in module lists (core vs advanced).  
2. Expand **Objective records to 3–5 measurable statements** per lesson; stop relying only on in-chapter objective lists.  
3. Map each quiz/qbank item to one primary objective + cognitive level.  
4. Add **Sources** footers with verified citations or `REFERENCE_REVIEW_REQUIRED`.  
5. Grow clinical cases toward multi-stage reasoning; do not mass-generate cases.  
6. Keep complementary foundation/clinical pairs; retitle same-level duplicates.  
7. Introduce a faculty **Medical content review** queue (not student-visible).

## Cardiovascular System (`mod-cv`)

### Current structure
Seed spine: Electrophysiology & Mechanics → IHD/ACS → HF therapy → (+ wave) Shock classification, Valvular hemodynamics, Tamponade, Atherosclerosis→rupture.  
Catalog adds: Electrophysiology Gradients, ACS path deep dive, HF mechanisms, Shock microcirculation, PH groups, coronary territories, etc.

### Recommended structure
1. **FOUNDATIONAL:** Cardiac cycle, hemodynamics, EP & mechanics  
2. **MECHANISM:** EP gradients / pacemaker currents; atherosclerosis→plaque rupture  
3. **DISEASE:** ACS pathophysiology; HF mechanisms (HFrEF / HFmrEF / HFpEF); valvular physics; pericardial constraints; PH definitions  
4. **CLINICAL (clerkship):** Chest pain & ACS reasoning; acute HF admission; shock at bedside  
5. **ADVANCED:** Complicated ACS ICU; ICU shock teams  
6. **CASE / ASSESSMENT:** Progressive dyspnea case; chest-pressure case; formative + qbank

### Consolidate
- Exact same-level overlap is limited; **Electrophysiology & Mechanics** vs **Gradients** → keep both; label Gradients as *advanced mechanism / antiarrhythmic map*.

### Split
- `Ischemic Heart Disease & ACS` could later split STEMI pathway vs NSTE-ACS risk stratification once reviewer-approved content exists (do not invent criteria now).

### Move
- Keep ICU ACS/shock topics in IM module but tag `ADVANCED_CLINICAL`.

### New cases (recommend only)
- Palpitations/syncope ECG reasoning  
- Tamponade obstructive shock  
- Decompensated HFrEF with GDMT reconciliation

### Missing concepts
- Explicit HFmrEF teaching sentence  
- Contemporary PH hemodynamic definition (verify before writing numbers)  
- Coronary circulation autoregulation lesson clarity

### Assessment opportunities
- Cognitive ladder: phase-0 recall → hyperkalemia mechanism → ECG interpretation → ACS diagnosis → next-best-step management

## Internal Medicine (`mod-im`)

### Current structure
Seed: CAP, chest pain/ACS reasoning, sepsis, etc.  
Catalog: dense Y3 ward topics + Y4 ICU/advanced layered in the same module list.

### Recommended structure
**CORE CLERKSHIP:** CAP, chest pain, HF admission, VTE/PE workup, AKI, electrolytes, GI bleed, DKA/HHS, sepsis recognition.  
**ADVANCED MEDICINE:** Hour-flexible sepsis reassessment, septic shock beyond initial resuscitation, complicated ACS ICU, oncologic emergencies (advanced), ICU goals of care.

### Consolidate / retitle
| Current | Recommendation |
| --- | --- |
| Community Pneumonia Disposition (×2) | Keep seed as disposition framework; retitle catalog to emphasize severity scores / IV→PO |
| Oncologic Emergencies (Y3 + Y4) | Retitle Y4 to “Oncologic Emergencies — ICU / Sub-I” |

### New cases (recommend only)
- Undifferentiated fever  
- Ward AKI  
- Non-variceal UGIB  
- Hyperkalemia already present — expand stages

### Assessment
- Shift catalog stems away from “which is most accurate?” toward diagnosis / next step / mechanism where content supports it.

## Clinical reasoning pathways (new — recommend only)

Do **not** mass-generate. Priority build order after review staffing:

1. Chest pain  
2. Shock  
3. Dyspnea  
4. Altered mental status  
5. AKI  
6. GI bleeding  
7. Fever  
8. Syncope  
9. Hyperglycemia  
10. Electrolyte abnormalities  

Each: immediate threats → initial assessment → differential → discriminators → investigations → interpretation → initial management → reassessment.

## What not to do automatically

- Delete advanced lessons because they are hard.  
- Invent guideline years, doses, or trial names.  
- Collapse foundation + clinical pairs into one lesson.  
- Claim `APPROVED` without a human reviewer.
